? XU LY LOI THUONG GAP:

  ? "python not found"
     ? Chua cai Python hoac chua tick "Add to PATH"
     ? Tai va cai lai tu python.org

     ? Chua cai thu vien
     ? Chay lai install.bat hoac install.sh

     ? File PowerPoint dang mo
