? TINH NANG BATCH MODE:

  ? Chon nhieu file:
     - Click "+ Them files" va chon nhieu file
     - Hoac click "+ Them folder" de them tat ca .pptx
  
  ? Progress bar:
     - Theo doi tien trinh real-time
     - Hien thi file dang xu ly
  
  ? Bao cao chi tiet:
     - Ket qua cho tung file
     - Danh dau files thanh cong / loi
  
  ? Tu dong backup:
     - Moi file duoc backup truoc khi xu ly
     - An toan tuyet doi


? VI DU SU DUNG:

  Case 1: Xoa logo tren 50 presentations
  
    1. Chay: run_batch.bat
    2. Click "+ Them folder"
    3. Chon folder chua 50 files
    4. Tab "Xoa hinh anh" > Cau hinh logo
    5. Click "Xu ly tat ca files"
    6. Doi 2-3 phut ? XONG 50 files!
    
    Thoi gian:
    - Thu cong: ~1 gio
    - Batch mode: 2-3 phut ?

  Case 2: Loc text 100 files song ngu
  
    1. Copy folder sang folder moi
    2. Chay batch mode
    3. "+ Them folder"
    4. Tab "Loc text" > "Giu tieng Viet"
    5. Xu ly ? XONG!
