================================================================================
                              LUONG XU LY
================================================================================

1. Nguoi dung nhap chu Han
2. Nguoi dung chon thu muc (neu muon)
3. Nguoi dung click "BAT DAU TAI"
4. Ung dung:
   - Phan tich input
   - Loai bo trung lap
   - Tai song song 5 anh cung luc
   - Hien thi tien trinh
   - Luu file vao thu muc
5. Hoan thanh:
   - Hien thi thong ke
   - San sang cho lan tai tiep theo
